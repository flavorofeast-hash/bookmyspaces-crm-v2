# BookMySpaces — Marketing Studio guide

Open **Marketing** in the CRM (phone or computer). Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge to make videos.

## Owner: one-time setup
1. **Settings → Properties**: check each property's *Phone numbers on promotions*, *WhatsApp*, *Location* and *Highlights* (real facilities only), then tick **I confirm these contact details are correct** → **Save**.
2. **Marketing → Photo gallery**: the 19 photos from www.bookmyspaces.in are already imported (exact originals). For each photo set the property and category, then tick **Approved for promotion** only if it is a true photo of the property.
   - **AI mark** badge (14 photos): the image has the Google Gemini sparkle, so it was made or edited with AI. Approve only if it truly shows the place as it is.
   - **Has text/price printed** (3 room banners with “₹999”): don't approve unless that price is still valid.
   - Upload your own real photos with **Upload photos** (JPG/PNG/WebP, a few at a time).
3. **Packages & Food**: approve items you want to advertise with a price. Promotions only show prices from approved items or set room rates; otherwise they say “ask us for current rates”.

## Daily: make and post a promotion (staff)
1. **Marketing → Promotion Studio** → choose the property.
2. Choose the type (rooms, rooftop, celebrations, dining, hall, café, seasonal), language (English, Bengali or both) and, if wanted, an approved offer → **Generate promotion**.
3. Review the image (square, portrait, story), the captions and the code (e.g. `MORT1A2B`).
4. **Download** the image, or **Make MP4 video** (15/30/45 s; 9:16, 1:1 or 16:9; built-in music, your own licensed music, or none) → **Download MP4**.
5. **Copy** the Facebook/Instagram caption, WhatsApp message, group post or classified listing.
6. Under **Where to post**, press **Open**, paste and upload by hand. Follow each group's rules. No bulk posting or unsolicited messages.
7. **Mark as posted**: choose where, paste the post link → **Mark as posted**.

The owner must press **Approve for posting** before a promotion can be marked as posted. Editing captions sends it back for approval.

## Plan a week
**Promotion Studio → Plan a whole week → Generate week** creates 7 drafts (one per day). They use different properties, types, wording and the least-used photos. The owner approves each one. Nothing is posted automatically.

## Free places to post
**Marketing → Free places to post** lists checked places with their rules. Google Business Profile and Bing Places are free. Justdial's rules are still UNKNOWN, so read its terms first. Use **Find more** to search Facebook groups yourself, read each group's rules, and add the group. Keep *Free posting* at UNKNOWN until you have read the rules.

## Results
Customers who message using a promotion's WhatsApp link or code are tagged automatically, along with the property. Staff can also tag a customer on the customer page (Campaign). **Marketing → Which promotions bring business** shows promotions, downloads, posts, enquiries, bookings and booked value per property and type. Views and likes are not shown; that data isn't available.

## WhatsApp
Inbox conversations show which property and promotion an enquiry came from. **Rates & packages** quick replies are built only from approved items. WhatsApp still runs on the old system (V3) until you move it (see the handover notes).
